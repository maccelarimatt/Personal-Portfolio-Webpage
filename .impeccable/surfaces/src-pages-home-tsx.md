# Surface brief: portfolio site (Home and floor pages)

Scope: the whole portfolio, led by Home (`src/pages/Home.tsx`); Research, Projects, About, Record (achievements) and Contact pages inherit the world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: PhD supervisors, collaborators and engineering hiring managers, arriving from a CV or LinkedIn, deciding in minutes whether to contact Matthew. Action: email him or download the CV. Proof: the real numbers in `src/data/` and the photos. Constraints: copy and facts unchanged; photos stay; form, filters and rotating titles may go.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one building of lit signs read from the street: every section is a signed floor and the directory is the navigation. It refuses the category default of a split hero over a row of glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Wet asphalt black ground, charcoal street, steel lines. Signs are flat backlit off-white acrylic panels with heavy condensed gothic caps in near-black; the active or open state snaps to neon red; lantern vermilion marks small labels and current work. No glow, no gradients except the street reflection, square corners. One sign module (floor cell, panel, spec cell) repeats as the page ruler.

STORY: Visitors read the directory, understand the research problem from the ground-floor sign in one line, see real numbers on each floor, and email Matthew or take the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: the vertical sign spine, 5F Contact (red) down to 1F Research, ending at a large ground sign carrying the name and research line, then a wet-street reflection of the spine. Right 5/12: the portrait in a lightbox frame, the NOW plate, a summary paragraph, a red "Email Matthew" mailto and an outlined "Download CV". The top bar repeats the floors.

FORM: Signboard Canyon (catalog challenger `vernacular-ephemera-signboard-canyon-midnight`, lead of the bolder re-roll round 1). seed:3ffa73ae1758565b. Raises: honest physical names on every label (from fluid ink basin); one identical sign module repeats as the ruler (from VU meter bridge). Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a floor lights it red and its reflection follows; motion grammar: the signs power on floor by floor once at load, ground first, with no motion under reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
